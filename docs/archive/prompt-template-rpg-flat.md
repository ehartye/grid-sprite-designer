## The Subject

The subject of your divine creation: **SHADOW WEAVER**.
A stealthy assassin with short-cropped black hair, pale skin, and narrow violet eyes that gleam in darkness. Slim, agile build with a low crouching ready stance.
**Equipment:** Form-fitting dark leather armor with deep purple trim, a half-face mask covering the lower face, two curved daggers with serrated edges, one dagger in each hand, and a belt with three throwing knives. A dark hooded cloak with a tattered hem.
**Color palette:** Black hair, violet eyes, pale skin. Very dark charcoal leather armor with deep purple accents. Daggers are dark steel with purple gem pommels. Cloak is near-black with purple lining.

Shadow Weaver's cloak is always visible. Her hood is always up. Her mask is always concealing the lower half of her face.

## Orientation

This is an RPG animation sprite sheet. Throughout the frame descriptions, we'll be using the following terms to indicate which direction the character is facing. Compass and Camera directions are from the perspective of the human viewer. Stage directions are from the perspective of the character.

**IMPORTANT**: Pay close attention to the orientation of the character

- **South**: Player Camera Down. Character Downstage Center. Toward the camera. When a character is "facing south", their full face and torso front are visible.
- **North**: Player Camera Up. Character Upstage Center. Away from the camera. When a character is "facing north", the back of their head and back are visible.
- **West**: Player Camera Left. Character Stage Right. When a character is "facing west", their left profile is visible.
- **East**: Player Camera Right. Character Stage Left. When a character is "facing east", their right profile is visible.
